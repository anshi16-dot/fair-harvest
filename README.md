# 🌾 Fair Harvest
### Better Prices for Farmers. Simpler Sourcing for Buyers.

**Fair Harvest** is a farmer-first digital marketplace designed to connect farmers across Northeast India with buyers directly. Our goal is to help farmers access fair market prices, reduce transportation costs, and reach more buyers through technology.

🌱 **Our Vision:** Making agricultural trade more transparent, accessible, and efficient.

---

## 🚩 The Problem

Farmers in Northeast India can face challenges such as:
- 💰 Limited access to transparent market prices.
- 🚚 High transportation costs.
- 🌾 Small harvest quantities that cannot meet bulk buyer demands.
- 📱 Language and digital accessibility barriers.

## 💡 Our Solution

Fair Harvest aims to solve these challenges through a simple, inclusive platform.

- 📊 **Transparent Pricing** — Access market price information.
- 🎙️ **Voice-Based Listing** — List crops using voice assistance.
- 🤝 **Harvest Aggregation** — Combine produce from multiple farmers.
- 🛒 **Direct Buyer Connection** — Connect farmers with potential bulk buyers.
- 🚛 **Shared Logistics** — Coordinate transportation to reduce costs.
- 🌐 **Regional Language Support** — Make the platform more accessible to local communities.

## 🔄 How It Works

1. 🌱 **List Produce:** Farmers enter their crop details, quantity, and location.
2. ✅ **Quality Check:** Produce is checked at a designated collection hub.
3. 🛒 **Find Buyers:** Buyers discover available produce and place orders.
4. 🚚 **Deliver Together:** Produce is collected and transported through planned pickup routes.

## 🎯 Our Target Users

- 👨‍🌾 Small and local farmers
- 👥 Farmer groups and cooperatives
- 🏨 Hotels and wholesale buyers
- 🚛 Local transport providers

## 🛠️ Technology

Our proposed platform can use technologies such as:

- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend:** Node.js or FastAPI
- **Database:** PostgreSQL
- **Voice Assistance:** Web Speech API or compatible speech services

*The final technology stack depends on the implementation.*

## 🌍 Our Impact

Fair Harvest aims to:

- Improve farmers' access to potential buyers.
- Promote transparent agricultural trade.
- Reduce per-unit transportation costs through shared logistics.
- Make digital agricultural services more accessible.
- Support a more connected agricultural ecosystem in Northeast India.
